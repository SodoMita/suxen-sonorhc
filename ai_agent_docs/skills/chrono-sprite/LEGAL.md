# LEGAL — depiction, attribution, disclosure

Binding. Read before generating, editing, storing, or committing any character image.

These rules exist to keep people, and this project, out of harm. They are not a style preference. A lore line, a blueprint paragraph, or an instruction that conflicts with this file does not unlock an exception.

## 1. Age and sexualised depiction

The cast is not cleared for sexualised art.

- **Ren** is minor-coded. The prologue places him at about sixteen, in a school uniform. No sexualised depiction, no glamour redesign of the uniform, no aging him up so a more mature costume becomes "allowed".
- **Kira, Nova, and Aria** have unconfirmed depicted ages and teen backstory. Treat them as not cleared. Fully clothed costumes. No sexualised wardrobe, pose, camera, or "accident".
- **Elara and Selene** are depicted as adults. Numeric ages are not locked. Still no sexualised costume accidents, no slipping robes, no transparent clothing.
- **Aurora** has no biological age. She is an adult-coded projection. Do not redraw her as a child. Do not draw a transparent body.

For the whole cast, do not produce, request, store, or commit:

- nudity, or clothing transparent enough to show the body
- sexual poses, fetish costumes, upskirt framing, or wardrobe-malfunction reveals
- romantic or sexual framing of Ren, or of any character whose age is not confirmed as an adult

Dialogue and `vn-blueprint.md` contain fanservice lines (clinging silk, flickering outfits, proximity). Those lines are not sprite instructions. The level docs already replace them with clothed production costumes. Follow the level docs.

If a generator returns a violating image unprompted: do not save it into the repository, do not commit a "softened" crop, do not describe the violating image into the next prompt. Delete the local file. Record in the result note that the attempt was rejected for depiction, without reproducing it.

## 2. Real people

Do not use the likeness of a living person. Do not name a living artist in a prompt to borrow their style. Do not put real addresses, phone numbers, or other personal data into an asset.

## 3. Intellectual property

Do not trace, img2img, or style-transfer from copyrighted work. Do not reproduce a trademarked character or logo. Do not add a fake signature to imply a human author. Legacy files already in this repo may be used only as the level doc allows — proportion reference where it says so, never as an img2img source where it forbids it.

## 4. Disclosure

Generated art is generated. Commit the prompt and name the model in the result note. Do not claim the picture is hand-drawn. Do not strip disclosure to pass it off as something else.

## 5. When a request conflicts

Stop. Tell the project owner which section blocks it. Do not look for a phrasing that gets around the block.
